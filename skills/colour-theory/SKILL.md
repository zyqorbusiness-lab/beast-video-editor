# Colour theory for edits

Reference evidence: every reel in `docs/reference-analysis/` runs a strict
accent discipline. This is what separates premium from noisy.

## The two-accent rule

1. **Brand accent** - the identity colour (barsol: red, edworld: orange,
   yunsan: pink, adito: purple). Spent on: emphasis words, key graphics,
   end cards, logo moments.
2. **System accent** (optional, at most one) - a second colour with one
   fixed job. edworld uses green only for the active caption word. It never
   touches branding.

Never introduce a third accent. Neutrals (white, black, greys, B&W photos)
are free.

## Contrast and legibility

- Captions: light text + dark outline/shadow, or dark text on a solid chip.
  Test on the busiest frame of the clip, not the average frame.
- Emphasis words: accent colour + a style change (italic serif, weight
  jump). Colour alone is too weak at caption sizes.
- Full-screen colour blocks: use the brand accent at full saturation, white
  or near-white type. Hold long enough to read twice (~1.5s minimum).

## Grading restraint

- Match footage to the palette, not the palette to footage. Desaturating
  supporting photos to B&W (barsol) keeps them subordinate.
- Colour-flash transitions use the accent or white. Never a random colour.
- Grade consistently across a scene; a grade change IS a transition signal.
