# Study: yunsan_studio - branded showreel wrapper

- Reel: https://www.instagram.com/yunsan_studio/reel/DfSwqnwTlCg/
- 1080x1920 @ 60fps, 17.88s, 537 frames viewed at 30fps sampling (9 sheets)
- Audio: 30 onsets, ~112.5 BPM estimate

## What it is

A studio showreel where client work plays inside a branded wrapper. The
wrapper is the lesson: the same footage would feel disposable without it.

## Techniques observed

- **Frame-within-frame packaging.** Every clip plays inside a rounded
  window on a pink sunburst background, with a "SHOWREEL" sticker header
  (with a small mascot mark). The wrapper never changes, so wildly
  different client clips feel like one body of work.
- **Fake editor-timeline progress bar.** A miniature editing-timeline
  graphic sits under the video window and advances like a playhead. It
  signals "this is crafted in an editor" and doubles as a progress meter.
- **Purple/magenta colour-flash transitions.** Segment changes ride a
  magenta flash, matching the pink brand family.
- **Diagonal stripe wipes.** Bold diagonal stripe wipes move between
  segments - a graphic alternative to the flash, used sparingly.
- **Animated subscribe/button elements.** UI elements (subscribe button,
  check/cross marks) animate in as small moments of delight.
- **"24 HOURS" glossy 3D text.** Pink glossy dimensional type for the
  service promise - the only 3D type moment, kept as a centrepiece.
- **"REACH US" end card.** Static card: heading, social icons row, email
  with a small cat mark, on the same pink sunburst. Held long.

## Numbers worth stealing

- Wrapper margins: the inner video window occupies roughly 80-85% of frame
  width, centred, with consistent corner radius
- End card hold: well over 3s (the reel ends on it)

## What this teaches the toolkit

Packaging is a skill. `skills/motion-graphics/SKILL.md` includes the
branded-wrapper pattern (background, window, header sticker, progress
element, end card) as a reusable composition.
