# Study: barsolmedia - podcast talking-head with caption system

- Reel: https://www.instagram.com/barsolmedia/reel/Dd1Mf7xzsbm/
- 1080x1920 @ 30fps (native), 26.77s, 803 frames (all viewed, 14 contact sheets)
- Audio: 41 onsets, ~62.1 BPM estimate (rough onset-density estimate)

## What it is

Podcast/talking-head clip elevated with a disciplined caption and overlay
system. The footage is ordinary; the edit is the product.

## Techniques observed

- **Word-by-word pop captions.** One word at a time, each popping in with a
  small scale-up. The active word is always the largest, brightest element.
- **Red italic serif emphasis words.** Key words switch to a red italic
  serif for a beat, then back to the base sans. One accent colour (red),
  spent only on emphasis.
- **Dotted world-map overlay with pins.** A full-width dotted map graphic
  drops in under the subject with location pins - context without cutting
  away from the speaker.
- **B&W photo + red accent numbers.** Supporting photos are desaturated to
  black and white, with red numerals (stats) on top. The B&W treatment keeps
  the photo subordinate to the speaker.
- **Full-screen colour-block typography.** The word "STANDARD" fills the
  frame on a solid red block - a palette break used as a punchline beat.
- **Logo outro hold.** 2-3s static logo hold at the end.

## Numbers worth stealing

- Caption pop: small scale-up over a few frames, then settle (estimate -
  perceived on contact sheets; per-frame scale values not measured)
- Emphasis word colour: single accent, italic serif, held for the word's
  spoken duration only
- Outro hold: 2-3s (estimate from contact sheets)

## What this teaches the toolkit

Talking-head work is a caption system plus a restrained overlay library.
The skill `skills/captions/SKILL.md` encodes the word-by-word pop timing;
`skills/motion-graphics/SKILL.md` covers the overlay patterns (map, B&W
photo cards, colour-block punches).
