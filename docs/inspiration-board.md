(@zyqorbusiness), three searches - "kinetic typography video",
"video editing transitions reel", "podcast captions motion graphics edit" -
plus one pin opened in detail. Per `docs/LICENSING.md`, no pin images are
saved into this repo: pins are other creators' work. This file keeps URLs +
what to borrow, which is the reference-only half of the workflow.
